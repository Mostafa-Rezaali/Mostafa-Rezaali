### About
I am a doctoral researcher in the Department of Geography at the University of Florida, working at the intersection of climate dynamics, earth system modeling, and artificial intelligence. My research focuses on advancing the detection, characterization, and subseasonal forecasting of extreme weather events—with a primary emphasis on heat wave dynamics, atmospheric teleconnections, and climate variability.

By combining observational and reanalysis datasets across the atmosphere, hydrosphere, and terrestrial biosphere with machine learning frameworks—including icosahedral mesh Graph Neural Networks (GNNs) and conditional generative modeling—I develop physics-informed, data-driven tools to unearth the complex physical mechanisms driving climate extremes. My goal is to bridge classical process-based climate modeling with modern geometric and generative AI techniques to improve predictive capabilities and support climate adaptation strategies.

---

### Key Research Areas
* **Dynamics of Climate Extremes:** Thermodynamic drivers, land-atmosphere feedbacks, soil moisture-temperature coupling, teleconnections, and atmospheric circulation patterns governing compound extremes.
* **Physics-Informed AI & Machine Learning:** Deep learning architectures (PyTorch, TensorFlow) including spherical/icosahedral Graph Neural Networks and conditional generative models for pattern recognition, extreme event detection, spatial downscaling, and subseasonal forecasting.
* **Earth System Analysis:** Diagnostic processing of high-resolution climate reanalysis datasets and ensemble forecasts (ERA5, GEFSv12, ECMWF IFS ENS, PRISM).

### Active Research Projects
* **Heat Wave Dynamics & Predictability (HeatCast):** Investigating physical drivers, teleconnections, and land-atmosphere coupling to enhance subseasonal heatwave forecasts using icosahedral mesh Graph Neural Networks (GNNs).
* **Earth System Model–AI Integration:** Constructing hybrid modeling frameworks that embed physical constraints into deep learning algorithms (including conditional flow matching and CVAEs) for subseasonal prediction.
* **Climate Analytics Framework:** Building reproducible, HPC-scalable workflows for processing multi-terabyte ensemble datasets and detecting compounding climate risk.
